Privacy Policy

PinPlay for Mac · Last updated November 8, 2026

What PinPlay collects

Nothing. PinPlay does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising code, and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

PinPlay remembers the card's position and size and the last video link you played, so it can restore them next time. This is saved only on your Mac, inside the app's private storage, and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Videos you play

When you play a video, PinPlay loads it from the video service using that service's own embedded player. The video service may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example YouTube under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

PinPlay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request.
